Water & power: what a hyperscale datacenter actually consumes

The short version

These facilities draw enormous amounts of water and electricity — the local questions are where it comes from and who pays.

In a nutshell

Hyperscale data centers can consume millions of gallons of water and hundreds of megawatts of power. For a rural county, the pressure lands on local watersheds, the grid, and ratepayers. The plain-language question isn't the engineering — it's whether the community absorbs the costs while the benefits leave.

  • Cooling can require large, sustained water withdrawals.
  • Power demand can rival that of a small city.
  • Infrastructure upgrades are often subsidized by the public.
