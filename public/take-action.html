How & where to submit a comment

Public comment is the record. Three steps:

  1. Find the open comment window — a Virginia DEQ permit comment period, or a Botetourt County Board of Supervisors agenda item. (We link the current one here when one is open.)
  2. Write two to four sentences in your own words using the talking points below. A personal reason — why this matters to you — is the most effective part.
  3. Submit before the deadline: through the DEQ public-comment portal, by email to the listed contact, or in person / by mail at the meeting. (Exact link and address added per comment period.)

Talking points (adapt them)

Copy, edit the bracketed parts in your own words, and submit. One personal sentence matters most.

How to make your voice heard

Decisions here are shaped by who shows up and speaks. Here's exactly how and where.

Submit a public comment

Public comments to the Virginia DEQ and the Board of Supervisors become part of the official record. Follow the steps above and use the talking points below.

Speak at a hearing

Board of Supervisors and permit hearings are open to the public. Sign up to speak (usually a few minutes each) — showing up in person carries weight. We post dates and sign-up details here.

Further reading — national context

We're not the first community to face this. These are national environmental-justice frameworks and a case study from the fight elsewhere. Unlike our own documents, these are external links — cited plainly with their source.

NAACP

Frontline Framework: Community Guiding Principles

Nine guiding principles for communities organizing against harmful data centers — health first, most-affected communities lead, jobs can't justify harm, enforceable accountability.

NAACP (PDF)

2026 Recommendations for Protecting Frontline Communities to Stop Dirty Data

A proactive playbook: call for moratoriums, end NDAs and closed-door deals, file public-records requests, demand ratepayer protections and that data centers bring their own clean energy.

CNBC · Jul 16, 2026

Musk's Memphis 'Colossus' AI center: epicenter of the data-center backlash

A cautionary case study: gas-turbine air pollution, unmet water-recycling promises, and rising utility bills in majority-Black Whitehaven — now the subject of an NAACP Clean Air Act suit.
